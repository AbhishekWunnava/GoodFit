How it works

Three steps, then a short list each day instead of hundreds of postings.

  1. Upload your resume

    We read your current role, company and years of experience, and you check what we found.

  2. Tell us what you want

    Roles, cities, work mode, pay, notice period and the kind of company you'd like.

  3. Open the jobs that fit

    Every opening is scored out of 100 on what the job really asks: the kind of PM role, must-haves, years in product, industry and B2B or B2C. Each point comes with a reason.
