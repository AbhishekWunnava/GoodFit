Your privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

The short version

Your resume and details are private to you. We use them only to find and score jobs for you. We never sell your data, never share it with employers, and never submit an application without your OK.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your resume: the file you upload, stored in private storage that only your account can open.
  • Your details and preferences: the role, company, experience and skills read from your resume (which you can edit), and the roles, cities, pay and other choices you set.
  • Saved and hidden jobs: kept in your browser on this device.
  • Match emails: if you turn them on, a record of when each email was sent.

Who helps us run GoodFit

  • Supabase stores your account, resume and details.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Anthropic (Claude) may read your resume to pull out your details, when AI reading is on. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Resend sends account and match emails.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

Jobs data

Job listings come from companies' public career pages. We don't log in to job sites or read your inbox.

Deleting your data

Uploading a new resume replaces the old file. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email hello@goodfitjobsonly.com from your account's email address. We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.
