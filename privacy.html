Your privacy

Last updated 28 November 2026

The short version

Your resume and details are private to you. We use them only to find and score jobs for you. We never sell your data, never share it with employers, and never submit an application without your OK. You can download or delete everything yourself, any time, on the Your data page. If you don't sign in for 12 months, we delete your account and its data.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your resume: the file you upload, stored in private storage that only your account can open.
  • Your details and preferences: the role, company, experience and skills read from your resume (which you can edit), your background (such as B2B or B2C and industries), and the roles, cities, pay and other choices you set.
  • Saved and hidden jobs: kept in your browser on this device, not on our servers.
  • Match emails: if you turn them on, a record of when each email was sent and how many jobs it listed.
  • The assistant: if you use "Ask GoodFit", we don't store your conversations. We keep only a count of how much you used it (number of messages and their size), to apply fair-use limits.
  • Technical logs: our hosting and database providers keep short-lived logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure.

Why we use it

  • To provide GoodFit to you: your account, resume, details and preferences are needed to find and score jobs for you.
  • Only when you choose to: AI reading of your resume, the assistant, and match emails are used only when you turn them on or use them. You can stop at any time.
  • To keep GoodFit safe: usage counts and technical logs help us prevent abuse and fix problems.

How matching works

GoodFit compares each job with your profile and gives it a score out of 100, with the reasons shown for every score. Scores only recommend jobs to you. No decision about you is made automatically, and nothing is sent to employers. If a score looks wrong, you can correct your details in your Profile.

Who helps us run GoodFit

  • Supabase stores your account, resume and details, and runs our server functions.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website.
  • Anthropic (Claude) reads your resume to pull out your details when AI reading is on, and answers your messages when you use the assistant. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.
  • Resend sends account and match emails.
  • GitHub runs the scheduled job that builds match emails from your preferences.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver the site's font and a code library, which means they receive your IP address when a page loads.

They process data only to provide these services to us, under their data processing terms. Some of them process data outside India, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and everything linked to it: until you delete it, or automatically after 12 months without signing in.
  • Records of match emails and assistant usage: 12 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Your resume: uploading a new one replaces and deletes the old file.
  • Saved and hidden jobs: in your browser until you clear its site data.

Your rights and choices

  • See and download your data: on the Your data page, as a file.
  • Correct it: edit your details and preferences in your Profile.
  • Delete it: on the Your data page. This deletes your account, resume, details, preferences and records straight away.
  • Stop optional uses: turn off match emails in Preferences, and stop using the assistant whenever you like.
  • Complain: if you're unhappy with how we handle your data, write to us first. You can also complain to the Data Protection Board of India, or, if you live in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

Security

Your data is stored with access rules that let only your account read it, your resume is in private storage, and secret keys are kept on our servers, never in the website. If a breach ever affects your data, we'll tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Jobs data

Job listings come from companies' public career pages and public job search services. We don't log in to job sites or read your inbox.

The GoodFit browser extension

The extension scores the job you're looking at on LinkedIn, Naukri, other job sites you turn on in the extension, or any page where you ask it to. It reads that one page only while you view it, scores it on your device with the same rules as the website, and never sends the page, the job or what you browse anywhere. It doesn't read your LinkedIn or Naukri messages, connections or profile, and it doesn't click, apply or post anything for you.

When you sign in, the extension gets your GoodFit profile (your resume details and preferences) from your account and keeps a copy, with your sign-in, in the extension's own storage on your computer. Signing out deletes both. The "Find people" links open LinkedIn's own search in your browser; the referral message is written on your device and you send it yourself.

Children

GoodFit is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you by email for important changes.

Questions

Write to hello@goodfitjobsonly.com. If you can't use the Your data page, email us from your account's email address and we'll delete your account within 7 days and confirm by email.
