GoodFit for Chrome

Your fit score, on every job you open.

Browsing LinkedIn or Naukri? GoodFit shows how well the job fits your resume, which must-haves your resume doesn't show yet, and who you could ask for a referral. Right there on the page.

How it works

It uses the profile you've already set up on GoodFit. Nothing new to fill in.

  1. Agree and install

    Read what the extension can see below, agree, and add it to Chrome. It also works in Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

  2. Sign in once

    Click the GoodFit icon and sign in with your GoodFit email and password. Your resume details come from your GoodFit profile.

  3. Open any job

    On LinkedIn and Naukri the score appears by itself. On any other job site, click the icon and choose Check this page.

What it reads, and what it never does

It reads

  • The job you open. Title, company, location and description of that one job, while it's on your screen, to score it.
  • Your GoodFit profile. The resume details and preferences you set up here, so it can compare.
  • Text you select, if you right-click it and choose "Check my fit with GoodFit".

It never

  • Sends the page, the job or your browsing anywhere. Scoring happens on your computer.
  • Reads your LinkedIn or Naukri messages, connections, profile or password.
  • Applies, clicks, messages or posts anything for you. You send every referral ask yourself.
  • Runs on other sites unless you ask it to on that page.

Questions

Will LinkedIn or Naukri know I'm using it?

The extension only reads what's already on your screen and doesn't send anything to them or click anything. It works like reading the page yourself.

Is the score the same as on GoodFit?

Yes. The extension uses the same scoring rules as the Matches page. If a job lists less detail on another site, the score can differ slightly and is marked "Limited info".

Firefox or Safari?

Not yet. It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other browsers built on Chromium.

How do I stop using it?

Click the GoodFit icon and Sign out, or remove it from chrome://extensions. You can also withdraw your agreement here, or on the Your data page.
